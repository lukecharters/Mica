# Mica Wiki

Mica (Mac Icon Creation App) is a native macOS app and command-line tool for creating customised app icons from SF Symbols and your own images — built for Mac Admins who need consistent, native-looking icons for self-service catalogues, scripts, and workflows.

## Where to start

- **[Getting Started](Getting-Started)** — install Mica, make your first icon, extract your first app icon.
- **[App Guide](App-Guide)** — every control in the app, explained: layers, generation modes, styling, previews, and export.
- **[CLI Guide](CLI-Guide)** — task-oriented guide to `mica-cli` with worked examples.
- **[CLI Reference](CLI-Reference)** — the complete reference for every command and flag.
- **[Extracting Icons](Extracting-Icons)** — pull icons out of apps and files, one at a time or in bulk.
- **[Colour Formats](Colour-Formats)** — every way to specify a colour, in the app and the CLI.

## Requirements at a glance

| Feature | Requires |
|---|---|
| Everything except the below | macOS 15 Sequoia or later |
| System generation mode (Liquid Glass icons) | macOS 26 Tahoe or later |
| Gradient symbol rendering | macOS 26 Tahoe or later |
